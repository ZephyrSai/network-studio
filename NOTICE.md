## Online icon search

Icons picked from the **Icons** tab are fetched from the public Iconify API at the time you pick them; each keeps its set id and licence in the drawing (`source`, `license`). Brand logos (SVG Logos, Simple Icons and similar sets) are trademarks of their respective owners and are provided for identification only.

## Imported packs

Shapes you import (vendor icon packs, SVG folders, draw.io libraries) stay in your browser and in drawings that use them. Make sure your use complies with the licence of the pack you import — for example, vendor network icon packs are typically licensed for use in diagrams but not for redistribution.
